# Mood board prompts for the "Create custom" sections

How to use: paste the **Base prompt** first, then add the **Section line** for the section you want. Generate one board per section and save them as `images/mood-<section>.jpg`. Send me the files (or screenshots) and I will put them on the section cards and behind the designer.

## Base prompt (same for every section)

> Create a mood board, one flat image, 4:5 portrait, for a handmade epoxy-resin art studio in Phuket, Thailand. Real photography only, no illustrations, no AI-looking gloss, no text or logos. Natural daylight, soft shadows, slightly imperfect hand-made surfaces. Palette: deep ocean blue, turquoise, sea-foam white, warm sand beige, copper and gold accents, a little coral. Layout: a loose collage of 6 to 9 photos with generous warm-cream gaps between them (colour #F4F0E9): one large hero photo, a few medium photos, and 2 or 3 close-up texture details (swirls of resin, crushed glass chips, gold leaf, sand and foam). Styling: linen, driftwood, shells, dried flowers, pale stone, a few green leaves. Mood: calm, warm, handcrafted, premium but not shiny.

## Section lines

- **Keychains & necklaces:** Add: small resin letters with tassels, a round sea pendant on a fine chain, a mermaid tail pendant, tiny starfish and pressed flowers set inside clear resin, keys on a linen cloth, a brass key ring.
- **Accessories:** Add: a resin bookmark with a silk tassel lying in an open book, a hair comb and a bun pin with dried flowers, a slim pen with gold leaf, on a pale wood desk.
- **Statues & decor:** Add: hand-poured domino and board-game pieces, a small resin lamp glowing warmly, a heart-shaped dish, sculptural pieces on a stone shelf.
- **Dining & restaurant:** Add: round cup stands with an ocean wave and a starfish, a wavy serving dish with pistachios and dried mango, a fruit bowl, a tea-light holder, an elegant set table with linen napkins and glassware, dark granite or white tablecloth.
- **Wooden cutting boards:** Add: a paddle-shaped teak cutting board with a blue resin wave and sand, a round wooden tray, an organic-shaped board, warm wood grain close-ups, olive branches and bread.
- **Any size canvas:** Add: a large sea-style resin canvas (deep blue water, white foam, sand, small shells and starfish) leaning on a white wall, an abstract canvas in plum and gold, a corner of a bright living room, close-ups of foam cells and metallic pigment.
- **Custom gifts:** Add: a photo collage set in resin, a name spelled in sand letters on an ocean pour, a gift box with ribbon, a handwritten tag, birthday and wedding styling in soft colours.
- **Send us a photo:** Add: a hand holding a printed photo next to a finished resin piece, sketches and colour swatches on a table, a phone showing a chat, kraft paper and pencils.

## Tips

- Generate at least 2 variations per section and pick the more natural one; keep the cream gaps so text can sit on top.
- For my backgrounds I use the first board of each section; tell me the file names and I will do the rest.
