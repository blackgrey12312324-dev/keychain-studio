# Style guide (private study notes, not for the shop)

Learned from reference photos Anna sent. These pictures are NOT part of the shop or any product. Only the rules below are used when the helper creates designs.

## Looks that work
- **Dip letters / tags / bookmarks:** solid pearl colour at the bottom melting into clear glass at the top, with a few gold (or silver for dark pieces) foil flakes in the clear part. Colours: baby blue, pink, white, mustard, navy/black, red. Some have a monogram or a tiny gold butterfly.
- **Round pendants:** half clear with gold foil along the dividing line, half pearly pink or white. One gold butterfly sticker.
- **Black + white + gold marble:** flowing white swirls through black with gold dust (organisers, coasters, boards). Gold rim on coasters.
- **Green + cream + gold:** emerald glass chips fading to cream, big gold flakes.
- **Sea letters / canvases:** navy to turquoise to white foam to sand, small shells or a starfish only on the sand. Dark sand or pebbles for a moodier version.
- **Abstract canvases:** a flowing teal (or any colour) river with parallel white lines and fine gold lines, cream or white open space, crushed-glass crystal clusters with gold leaf in a corner.
- **Boards:** resin on the handle and upper part, a flowing marble edge (diagonal or wavy, never ruler-straight), white, grey/beige and a little black or gold, cells and fine lace. Colourful versions: blue, green, pink, black and gold.
- **Coffee:** clear resin with coffee beans and gold leaf, or a taupe/cream marble round platter with beans, a broken white cup and a gold spoon.
- **Floral cubes:** pressed or dried flowers (purple, yellow, pink, white) in clear glass; baby's breath for a light look.

## Rules
1. One metal for foil per piece (gold OR silver), never several glitter colours.
2. Few big foil flakes look better than lots of fine random glitter.
3. Foil goes in the clear area or along a colour edge, not everywhere.
4. Dark pieces take gold or silver, pastel pieces take gold.
5. Charms or flowers sit in the clear area, never on top of a solid colour.
6. Wooden pieces keep a visible wood part, with a soft flowing resin edge.

## More references (second batch)
- **Dominoes:** gold domed pips and a thin gold divider; pearl-white, cream marble or deep blue bodies; two-tone blue and pearl-white with gold flakes where the two colours meet; black with gold pips.
- **Tic-tac-toe:** white pearl board with scattered gold flakes, solid white X, clear O rings full of gold leaf. Blue and clear version with gold.
- **Chess:** blue and clear squares with gold flakes and tiny moon or star charms, clear gold-flaked pieces against blue pieces; black and clear with red or rose flakes.
- **Combs and clips:** clear glass with white daisies, small green leaves and gold flakes; or red, purple and yellow dried flowers with gold flakes.
- **Hearts:** clear with white chips and a few gold flakes. **Letters:** cream marble swirl on top, clear below with chunky gold leaf.
- **Bookmarks and feathers:** clear to ocean blue with baby's breath; pink pearl feather with gold flakes at the top; pale blue crackle pens with gold flakes.
- **Galaxy tile:** black with a teal nebula and tiny white star specks.

## Third batch
- **Sunset sea canvases:** pink / orange / magenta sky blending into a white foam wave over pale sand, optionally two tiny turtles or dark rocks. Not only blue.
- **Boards:** black + white + gold marble with cells on bamboo; navy to turquoise wave with white foam on walnut, the wave edge diagonal and wavy. A gold line runs along the foam edge. Coasters in the same palette. Surfboard shapes also work.
- **Framed beach:** dark sea, turquoise waves with lace foam, white sand, small rocks, a tiny boat, green moss.
- **Galaxy tiles:** black base, teal or blue/purple nebula, tiny white stars.
- **Marble coffee platter:** taupe and cream swirl with beans and a gold spoon.

## Fourth batch (coasters, trays, kitchen)
- **Round coasters:** half solid pearl colour (pink or royal blue), half clear glass, with gold flakes concentrated along the line where the colour dissolves into the clear. A matching holder in the same colours.
- **Oval tray:** white pearl at the rim fading into ocean blue with fine cell veins, a few gold leaf pieces on the line between white and blue.
- **Coasters with fruit:** dried orange slices, berries and gold leaf in clear or amber or black resin, with a thin gold rim and a wavy edge.
- **Boards:** teal/navy/gold fluid on bamboo with a ragged resin edge and matching round coasters; wooden tools and bowls with blue sea only on a part.
- **Rule:** the colour/clear split with gold flakes on the dividing line is the strongest pattern across coasters, trays, keychains and dominoes.

## Fifth batch (coasters)
- **Black and white coasters:** a glossy black half and a clean white half, joined by a flowing band of grey marble with gold leaf lines. Very few colours.
- **Pressed-flower coasters:** clear round discs with baby's breath or forget-me-not sprigs (pink, blue, purple) arranged as a branch, with a few gold flakes near the edge. Pairs well with coffee.
- **Rule:** one clean split (colour / clear, or black / white) with gold on the dividing line looks more expensive than busy full-surface patterns.
