## Prompt

I run Resincraftcode, a handmade resin art studio in Phuket. I uploaded
two PDFs: one with ALL my molds, colours, glitters and the rules for what
can go in each mold, and one with ALL my real add-ons.

Create 12 inspirational keychain and accessory designs that I could
really make. Use ONLY the molds, colours, glitters and add-ons from my
PDFs. Follow the rules in the PDF exactly:

- Never change the shape of a mold or an add-on. You may only improve
  image quality (sharper, clean edges, remove grey halo, soft realistic
  shadow).
- Small and thin molds: only little figures, pressed flowers, metal
  charms, sea animals, real shells/starfish. No crowns, trees, paper
  flowers, lights, dried flowers or cones.
- Medium molds: also dried flowers, cones/pods, lights, crowns, little tree.
- Big molds: also paper flowers, roses and big trees.
- Gradient style: colours + glitter only, nothing inside.
- Ink dye style: only little figures, sea animals, charms, pressed and
  dried flowers inside.
- Sea style: fixed blue water and sand, ONLY real shells and starfish,
  no glitter, no other colours.
- Use only the colours and glitters listed. No rainbow, no iridescent.
- Each design: clear glossy resin, gold/silver/rose-gold ring and chain
  where the mold allows it, optional tassel from my tassel colours.
- Look like a real photo of a handmade product on a soft cream, pale
  blue or peach background. Calm, elegant, uncluttered. Not AI-looking.
- Under each design write: mold name, style, colours, glitter, add-ons.

Mix the sections: 4 keychains (letters/hearts), 2 sea designs,
2 bookmarks or pens, 2 cup stands or trays, 1 hair pin, 1 wooden board.
Add the small text "Resincraftcode" at the bottom of each page.
